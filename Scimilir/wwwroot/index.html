Your models

Get a model

Models come from Hugging Face, a free library. Try one of these to start, or search. Pick a file whose size fits in your graphics card's memory, with room to spare; files marked Q4_K_M are a good balance of quality and size.

Advanced

OpenRouter gives you hundreds of cloud models (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Llama and more) with one account. Your messages go over the internet to the company that runs the model, and most models cost a little per message.

  1. Make an account at openrouter.ai and add some credit (some models are free).
  2. Go to openrouter.ai/keys, press Create Key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and press Save.

  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. Scan the code with your phone's camera, or type the address into its browser.
  3. Log in with your Scimilir password.

Doesn't load? The first time Scimilir starts, Windows asks whether to allow it on networks: tick Private networks. If you missed it, search the Start menu for “Allow an app through Windows Firewall” and tick Private next to “dotnet” or “Scimilir”. Your Wi-Fi should also be set as a Private network in Windows.

Tip: in your phone browser's menu, choose “Add to Home screen” to open Scimilir like an app.

Changing it logs out every other phone and browser.
